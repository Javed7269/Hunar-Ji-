Privacy Policy

How HUNHAR JI handles personal information and protects account data.

Effective date: 27 November 2026
Platform: HUNHAR JI mobile application and related websites.

Our privacy commitment: HUNHAR JI does not sell personal information. Personal data is not made public or shared with unrelated people. Information is accessed only for the purposes described below, by authorised personnel and service providers who need it to operate the platform, or where disclosure is legally required. No online service can promise absolute security, but we apply access controls and safeguards appropriate to the information handled.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, mobile number, email address, role and authentication identifiers.
  • Service and order details, including items selected, booking history, delivery instructions, support requests and transaction references.
  • Address and location details that you choose to provide or enable, including current location when you request location-based services.
  • Partner onboarding and KYC information, which may include identity, business, bank or supporting documents when required for verification.
  • Device, diagnostic, security and usage information needed to keep the app reliable, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; match customers with service providers; process orders, bookings and delivery; verify partner applications; provide support; prevent fraud; maintain records; improve the platform; and meet legal obligations. We use location only for features that need it, such as finding nearby services or setting a delivery/service address.

3. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal data or allow unrelated users to browse private account records. Information may be disclosed only where needed:

  • To complete a request: limited contact, job, order or delivery details may be shown to the customer, assigned professional, seller or delivery partner involved in that specific request.
  • To operate the platform: trusted infrastructure, hosting, authentication, messaging, maps, analytics or payment providers may process the minimum information needed to provide their service, subject to their terms and applicable safeguards.
  • For legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed when required by law, a valid legal process, or to protect users, the platform and public safety.

We do not share KYC documents, private identity details, passwords, payment credentials or precise location with other users except where a specific lawful and necessary workflow requires limited information. Access to sensitive records must be restricted to authorised roles.

4. Payments and financial information

Where a third-party payment provider is enabled, payment details are handled by that provider under its own privacy and security terms. HUNHAR JI should retain only necessary transaction identifiers, status and reconciliation information. Never send card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords or one-time passcodes to support staff.

5. Advertising, banners and analytics

If advertising features are enabled, the app may display platform-managed banners or ads from providers such as Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, approximate technical information and ad interaction data under their own privacy terms. HUNHAR JI should use test ads during development, configure consent and age-appropriate settings before production, avoid using KYC or sensitive account information for ad targeting, and provide available choices for personalised advertising. Admin-managed banners should use only content approved for the platform. Third-party ad settings must not override user privacy choices or applicable law.

6. KYC and document security

KYC information is used for partner verification, fraud prevention and legal compliance. It should be stored in private storage, not in public image links. Access and downloads must be limited to authorised reviewers and protected by server-enforced access checks. Documents should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose or legal requirements.

7. Security measures

HUNHAR JI uses or plans to use authentication, role-based access controls, database security rules, private document storage, secret management, secure connections, audit records and least-privilege access. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Users should protect their device and account credentials and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, resolve disputes, maintain financial and security records, and comply with law. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and lawful retention requirements. Some transaction, fraud-prevention or compliance records may need to be retained after account closure.

9. Your choices and rights

You may update account details, manage device permissions such as location, and contact the platform to request access, correction or deletion. You can withdraw optional permissions in your device settings; some features may then stop working. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable Indian data protection and consumer laws.

10. Children

The platform is not intended for children to create accounts independently. A parent or lawful guardian should contact the platform if a child's information appears to have been submitted without appropriate consent.

11. Third-party services and links

The app or website may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before using external services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will show a revised effective date. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate in-app or website notice.

13. Privacy contact and grievance requests

Before public launch, the operator must publish and maintain a monitored privacy/grievance contact channel and business identity on this page. Until those details are formally supplied and verified, please use the official project contact channel provided by the platform operator. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment PINs in a privacy request.

This policy describes intended platform practices and must be checked against the final live app, actual service providers, retention schedule and applicable legal requirements before public launch. It is not a substitute for legal review.
